QQ空间博物馆

藏品 QQ空间 关于

关于

这是一座关于 QQ 空间装扮的电子博物馆，挖掘并收藏 2005 至 2009 年间的 5,863 件装扮，包括皮肤、挂件、漂浮物、鼠标指针、标题栏与 Flash 音乐播放器。

QQ 空间是腾讯于 2005 年推出的个人主页平台，鼎盛时期月活跃用户超过六亿，是当时中国最大的社交网络。对一代人来说，它是人生中第一个自己的页面，Y2K 极繁主义和"非主流"在中文互联网的模样。

互联网与记忆

Adobe 在 2020 年底终结了 Flash，浏览器随之将其移除；QQ 秀于 2021 年下架；花藤在 2022 年停止运营；虽然 QQ 空间依然在线，装扮商城也已不复存在。

本项目收藏的所有素材都来自 Internet Archive 和腾讯仍在线的 CDN。

  • 项目的开始，我半信半疑地从 Internet Archive 时光机的 CDX 索引出发，寻找出所有曾被存档的装扮素材地址，并发现绝大多数素材在十五年后仍能以原始地址访问。
  • 于是我优先从原始地址直接取回素材，少数失效的再从时光机恢复。物品的编号、中文名、上架日期、价格与色系标签，全部还原自 QQ 空间装扮商城的目录接口。
  • 动画鼠标文件被逐帧解码为现代浏览器能播放的 APNG 格式，播放器与 Flash 挂件由 Ruffle 模拟器运行，漂浮物轨迹还原自 2006 年腾讯的前端代码。

中文互联网很早就整体搬进了封闭平台，可时光机无法抓取需要账号才能看见的内容。QQ 空间里以文件形态存在的 GIF、皮肤、鼠标，都意外地活了下来；而在登录接口之后的数据，像用户的布局、花藤、留言板，却和服务器一起消失了。

About

This is a digital archive of QZone (QQ空间) decorations, collecting 5,863 files from between 2005 and 2009, including skins, pendants, floaties, cursors, title bars and flash music players.

QZone is the personal-homepage platform Tencent launched in 2005. At its peak it had over 600 million monthly active users, making it the largest social network in China at the time. For a generation it was the first page of their own, and what Y2K maximalism and "non-mainstream" looked like on the Chinese internet.

On internet memory

Adobe ended Flash at the close of 2020 and browsers removed it; QQShow was taken down in 2021; the flower-vine garden stopped operating in 2022; though QZone itself technically still exists, its decoration mall is long gone.

Everything in this collection comes from the Internet Archive and from Tencent's still-online CDNs.

  • The project started from the Internet Archive Wayback Machine's CDX index, where I found out that most of the decoration assets that were archived still answered at their original addresses, fifteen years later.
  • So I fetched assets straight from their original URLs, then recovered the few dead ones from the Wayback Machine. The item ids, Chinese names, release dates, prices and color tags were all crawled from the QZone decoration mall's catalog API.
  • The animated cursor .ani files were decoded frame by frame into APNGs, the music players Flash pendants ran in the Ruffle emulator, and the floaties' drifting paths were reconstructed according to Tencent's 2006 front-end code.

The Chinese web moved into closed platforms early and wholesale, and the Wayback Machine cannot crawl content behind an account. The parts of QZone that survived were files like the GIFs, skins, and cursors; meanwhile, the data that lived behind login endpoints — custom layouts, flower gardens, guestbooks — all disappeared along with the servers.

Acknowledgements

This project is deeply influenced by my friend Kay Zeng's qqshow2000.com. She rebuilt QQShow from assets salvaged out of the Wayback Machine; her site also lives inside this museum's QZone dress-up page. Like QQShow, QZone shaped how hundreds of millions of people first imagined the internet, yet remains nearly unknown outside the Chinese-speaking web. We hope this history can be seen by the wider world.

Equally formative were Jordan Eldredge's Webamp and Winamp Skin Museum, which brought the Windows desktop music player back to life by rewriting it in JavaScript. And thank you to artist friends doing internet digital-archive work, including Yufeng Zhao and Lewei Huang's BannerDepot 2000 and Amad Ansari's Palestine Online.

  • The QZone Museum is designed and built by viola he with the help of claude code, who first learned html through QZone and blogbus.
  • Typefaces: Zpix, LXGW ZhenKai
  • Artwork: the mostly unnamed Tencent designers who drew these 5,863 pixel pieces. If your work is here, I'd love to hear from you!
