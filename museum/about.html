关于About

这是一座关于 QQ空间装扮 的博物馆，收藏 2005 至 2009 年间的皮肤、挂件、 漂浮物、鼠标、标题栏与音乐播放器，共 5,863 件。

A museum of QZone decorations, collecting the skins, pendants, floaties, cursors, title bars and music players that Chinese teenagers used to dress up their homepages between 2005 and 2009, with a totle of 5,863 files.
